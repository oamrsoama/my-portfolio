❓ السؤال ✅ الإجابة
Why was Fogg arrested in Calcutta? Because Passepartout had entered a Hindu temple with his shoes on.
How did Fogg secure their freedom in court? He offered bail — he placed £2,000 on the judge's desk.
What did Fix do to Passepartout in Hong Kong? He drugged Passepartout in an opium den to delay Fogg.
How did Fogg cross from Hong Kong to Shanghai? He hired a small schooner called the Tankadere for £100 per day plus £200 extra.
Why did Fix travel on Fogg's ticket across the Pacific? Because the British arrest warrant would be invalid in San Francisco, so he needed Fogg to succeed to get back to England.
How did Fogg react when he found out Fix was a detective? He stayed calm and ignored it — he said Fix was just doing his job.
Why did Fogg go into the wilderness in Chapter 10? To rescue Passepartout who was captured by the Sioux warriors.
How did they cross the snowy plains after losing the train? Using a wind-sledge (a small sail-driven vehicle) that crossed the snow-covered plains rapidly.
How did Fogg convince the captain of the Henrietta? He bribed the crew and temporarily incapacitated the captain to take control of the ship.
Why did Fogg burn parts of the Henrietta? To conserve coal and use the wood as fuel to speed up crossing the Atlantic.
Why did Fix arrest Fogg in Liverpool? He still believed Fogg was the Bank of England robber and had a warrant for his arrest.
What was Fix's mistake? The real Bank of England robber had been caught in Brighton — Fogg was innocent all along.
How did Fogg react to Fix's mistake? He showed no anger and released Fix calmly, preparing for the final leg of his journey.
How did Fogg complete the journey in 79 days instead of 80? By crossing the International Date Line eastward, they gained a day — so they had one extra day.
What time did Fogg arrive at the Reform Club? At 8:44 p.m. — completing the journey in 79 days, 23 hours, and 59 minutes.
Who did Fogg marry at the end? Aouda — he proposed the next day after returning to London.
How do you think Fix feels when Fogg pays bail? He feels frustrated because Fogg escapes again and the merchants arrived too late to testify.
If you were Passepartout, would you help Fix arrest Fogg? Why? No, because Fogg is loyal, generous, and has shown great courage. He risked his life and money to help others. He deserves trust and loyalty in return.
