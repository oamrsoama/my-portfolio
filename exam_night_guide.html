Unit 7 — Conditionals (الجمل الشرطية)

1st Conditional — المستقبل المحتمل

If + Present Simple, will + Verb
If you study hard, you will pass.
If it rains, we will stay home.

2nd Conditional — غير واقعي (الحاضر)

If + Past Simple, would/could/might + Verb
If I were a bird, I could fly.
If I were you, I'd help him.

3rd Conditional — ماضٍ لم يحدث

If + had + P.P., would/could/might + have + P.P.
If they had known, they would have visited.
Had you finished early, you'd have watched the film.

بدائل If — Alternatives

Unless = If not (لو لم يكن)
I can't buy it unless I have money.
Provided / As long as = بشرط أن
He can play provided he finishes first.
Without + n/v.ing
You won't succeed without studying.
But for + Noun
But for doctors, we might die.

تحويلات الجمل الشرطية

Should + Subject + Inf. → 1st
Should you take this medicine, you'll feel better.
Were + Subject + to Inf. → 2nd
Were he to travel, he'd be sad.
Had + Subject + P.P. → 3rd
Had you finished early, you'd have watched the film.
Unit 8 — Modal Verbs (الأفعال الناقصة)

Necessity — الضرورة ✅

Must — ضرورة ذاتية من نفسك
I must buy a gift (أنا اللي قرر).
Have to / Has to — ضرورة خارجية
I have to wait (الدكتور لم يأت بعد).
Had to — ماضي Have to
He had to find a job to support his family.

Prohibition — الحظر ❌

Mustn't — حرام / ممنوع
You mustn't drive fast. (ممنوع)
You mustn't smoke in hospitals.
It's against the law.

Lack of Necessity — عدم الضرورة

Don't / Doesn't have to — مش لازم
She doesn't have to buy many dresses.
Needn't — لا ضرورة (وقت الكلام)
You needn't hurry.
Didn't have to — لم يكن ضرورياً ولم يفعله
Needn't have + P.P. — فعله لكن لم يكن لازم

Advice — النصيحة 💡

Should / Ought to + Verb
You should study harder.
You ought to listen carefully.
Shouldn't = نصيحة بعدم الفعل
You shouldn't eat junk food.

⚠️ الفرق المهم: Must vs Have to vs Mustn't vs Don't have to

Must = يجب (ضرورة ذاتية قوية)
Have to = يجب (من الخارج)
Mustn't = لا يجوز / حرام تفعل
Don't have to = لست مضطراً (مش لازم لكن ممكن)
🔁 Rewriting Sentences — إعادة الصياغة

جمل الاحتمال (If)

Hala doesn't hurry. (If...)
→ If Hala hurried, she would catch the train.
People aren't allowed to smoke here. (mustn't)
→ People mustn't smoke here.

Should / Were / Had

If you take this medicine → Should you take this medicine...
If I were you → Were I you, I wouldn't...
If he had known → Had he known...
